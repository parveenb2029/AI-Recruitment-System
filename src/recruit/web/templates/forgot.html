Forgotten your password?

This system cannot send email. There is no reset link, because nothing here is able to send you one. A page promising a message that never arrives would waste an hour of your day before you worked out it was lying, so here is the truth instead.

There are two ways back in, depending on who you are.

If someone else administers this

Ask them. It takes them about ten seconds: they open Team, find your name and choose Reset password. A new one appears on their screen once, and they read it to you. Change it after you sign in.

If that person is you

You need the machine this is running on. Two options:

  1. Open this page on that computer, at http://127.0.0.1:8000/forgot, and it will let you set a new password directly.
  2. Or run this where the application is installed:
    python -m recruit.users set-password you@example.com

Neither is offered over the network on purpose. A password reset that anybody who can reach the address may use is not a password.
