These are recordings, not live runs. Both extractions were produced by running the real pipeline over that resume, then saved, so this page loads instantly and shows the same thing every time. The misread address is not staged for effect — a small model running on a laptop genuinely produced it, and the check that catches it here is the same code that runs on a real document.

Why it works this way

A computer reading a CV will sometimes invent a job, and will more often misread a real one. So it is never allowed to simply assert something — it has to quote the document, and what it quotes is checked against what it wrote down.

Who decides

A person does, every time. The system ranks, explains and flags. It never rejects anybody on its own, and every decision is written to a log that nobody can edit afterwards.
