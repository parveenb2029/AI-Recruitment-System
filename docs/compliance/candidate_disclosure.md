### How we review your application

We use software that includes artificial intelligence to help us review
applications for this role. We want you to know exactly what it does.

**What the software does**

- Reads your CV or resume and pulls out your work history, education, skills,
  and certifications.
- Compares what it found against the requirements listed in the job description.
- Produces a summary and a match score, with a quote from your CV supporting
  each conclusion.

**What it does not do**

- **It does not decide whether you are hired, interviewed, or rejected.** Every
  decision about your application is made by a person.
- It does not use your name, age, gender, nationality, address, or the prestige
  of your school as part of the score. Those are explicitly excluded.
- It does not look at your social media or any source you have not given us.

**When a person looks at your application**

Always. A member of the recruitment team reviews the software's summary against
your actual CV before any decision. Where the software is unsure about
something, that is flagged for closer attention.

**If you would rather not be assessed this way**

You can ask for your application to be reviewed without the AI step. It will not
count against you. Contact `[ORGANIZATION TO COMPLETE: email]` and we will
arrange a manual review.
